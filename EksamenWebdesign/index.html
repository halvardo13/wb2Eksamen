What We Do

Our Mission

Our core mission is the protection and preservation of forests and trees, all around the globe. As climate change threatens, the forest biomes and its wildlife will continue to be threatened.

We work everyday to make a change, and keep the nature how we know it.

How we operate

We are a non-profit organization, and donations from like-minded people are what keeps us running! The money we get is used for a varied number of initiatives, that is always centered around preserving forests.
